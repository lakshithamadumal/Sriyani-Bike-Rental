Terms & Conditions

Bike Condition

The bike will be handed over in good riding condition after inspection, with a full fuel tank. The renter must inspect the bike before accepting it.

Rental Time & Charges

Rental time is from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM . The charge for this period is LKR 2,500 . Usage after 10:00 PM will be charged on an hourly basis.

Documents & Verification

A valid driving license accepted in Sri Lanka is mandatory and will be checked before issuing the bike. The renter must sign the rental agreement.

Safety Gear

A helmet is provided with the bike. The renter is responsible for wearing and properly using safety gear during the rental period.

Fuel Policy

The bike is provided with a full fuel tank at the time of handover. The renter must return the bike with a full fuel tank. If the bike is returned without a full tank, the renter must pay the cost of the missing fuel as determined by Sriyani Bike Rental.

Damage & Liability

Any damage, loss, or missing parts during the rental period must be fully paid by the renter. Sriyani Bike Rental is not responsible for accidents, injuries, traffic violations, or legal issues caused by the renter.

Important Notes

The bike is issued only after verifying the driving license.

Late returns may result in additional charges.

The renter must ride responsibly and follow Sri Lankan traffic laws.
